# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Arcivo community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use Arcivo
- Protecting other people's privacy – never post someone's messages, phone number or session data

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, sexualised language or
imagery, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/sadult/arcivo/security/advisories/new) or the contact listed on
the maintainers' GitHub profiles. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces.
