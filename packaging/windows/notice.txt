Arcivo – Your Saved Messages, organised.

Before you install, please note:

• Arcivo is an independent, open-source application. It is NOT made, endorsed or
  supported by Telegram. It uses the official Telegram API.

• You will sign in with your own api_id and api_hash, created at
  https://my.telegram.org/apps. Your session is stored in the Windows credential
  vault (DPAPI) on this computer and is never sent anywhere except Telegram.

• Arcivo keeps a local index of your Saved Messages so you can search, analyse and
  export them. Nothing is uploaded to third parties, and your data is never used
  to train AI models.

• Arcivo never deletes or changes anything on Telegram without your explicit
  confirmation. Deletions on Telegram cannot be undone.

You can uninstall at any time; you will be asked whether to keep your local data.
